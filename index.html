About Me

Hi! I am a PhD student at the University of Michigan School of Information, advised by Daniel Romero and Ceren Budak. Broadly, my research lies at the intersection of computational social science and human-computer interaction. In particular, I am interested in understanding the impacts of large language models on individuals and online social interactions.

Before my PhD, I was a lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at King Saud University.
